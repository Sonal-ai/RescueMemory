Implementation notes +
01 / ON THE PHONE

Offline chat currently searches bundled guidance in JavaScript; local vectors also support facility recommendations. IndexedDB stores reports and imported guides.

02 / NEARBY TRANSFER

Android BLE discovers another RescueMemory phone and sends a compact SOS packet. QR is the browser fallback.

03 / RESPONSE NODE

FastAPI handles connected HTTP sync. Qdrant Edge runs in the Python backend; Qdrant Server mirroring is optional.

Native Qdrant Edge retrieval on the phone is planned. This diagram shows the current implementation. End-to-end responder delivery still requires a working link and deployment configuration.

is uncertain.

The essentials from your phone to the people coordinating a response.

01 / RESILIENCE

Knowledge that stays.

Emergency guidance travels with the app. Reports are stored locally while a connection is unavailable, ready for a later sync.

02 / VISIBILITY

Share with intention.

Choose public or responder visibility for reports. Share community hazards and resources, and use responder workflows for sensitive details.

03 / TRUST

Guidance with a source.

Reference cards include sources. Authorized admins can publish signed protocols and checkpoint directives through the response node.
